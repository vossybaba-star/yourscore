Fantasy Premier League just told you the best striker in the league costs a sixth of your budget. Pick, or tax?

FPL revealed its 2026/27 prices last week and made Erling Haaland the most expensive player in its history at £15.5m. That's the headline. What they changed underneath it matters more.

**The quick version:**

- Haaland's a record £15.5m. Spend it and £84.5m covers your other fourteen.
- The prices aren't the news. The rule changes are.
- Neither touches the one thing that actually caps your season.

## £15.5m is a tax, not a pick

Fair prices do your thinking for you. The obvious players cost what they're worth, so everyone builds the same premium spine and your edge has to come from somewhere the price list can't see.

The community called it two days early: with Salah gone, FPL had to price Haaland up or he sits in 100% of teams. The guess was £15.0m. FPL went further.
