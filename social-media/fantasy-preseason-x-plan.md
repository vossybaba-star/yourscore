## 1. The job

FPL preseason is the loudest fortnight in football social. Everyone is posting drafts,
arguing about template teams and moaning about the same three broken rules they moan about
every year. We are not going to out-shout FPL. We are going to sit inside their arguments
and be the answer to them.

Every complaint about FPL is an ad for us. That's the whole strategy.

**Goals, in order:**
1. Waitlist signups (CTA: yourscore.app, gameweek-1 access, launches Friday 21 August)
2. Replies and quote posts (debate is the metric, not likes)
3. Follows (we are building a community, not running a campaign)

## 2. Voice

London. Direct. Zero corn.

- Short sentences. Full stops do the work.
- Say the thing. No "we're thrilled to announce". No rocket emojis. No hashtag walls.
- Confident, not arrogant. We take shots at FPL's rules, never at FPL players.
- Never punch down at a user's squad. Rate it honest, keep it funny, never nasty.
- Hashtags: none, or one max when it earns its place. It's 2026.

**Locked vocabulary (non-negotiable, from YOURSCORE.md):**
- "football knowledge", never "football IQ"
- "correct answers earn transfers", NEVER "every answer banks a transfer"
- No em or en dashes in post copy. Commas and full stops.
- "friends", not "mates"
- It's "YourScore Fantasy" or "Fantasy PL". The quiz that earns transfers is the weekly
  knowledge round.
- No cash or prize promises. All giveaways are retired (founder, 13 Jul). Do not invent one.
- No fake engagement stats, ever. The old brief's "only 11% got this right" tactic is dead.
  If we quote a number it's real.

## 3. The six pillars (what makes us different, one argument each)

Everything we post preseason maps to one of these. Each pillar = a recurring argument we
own, with receipts from the actual product.

### P1 · Know more, move more (the flagship)
FPL hands the person who watches every minute and the person who forgot the season started
the same one free transfer. We don't. Each gameweek there's a knowledge round: correct
answers earn transfer credits (5+ right earns 1, 7+ earns 2, 9+ earns 3, a perfect 11
earns 4, bank caps at 5). Your football brain is the currency. Extra moves still cost 4
points, same as the rules you already know.

### P2 · No bonus lottery
No BPS. Ever. Every point traces to something that actually happened in the match.
No algorithm quietly handing your striker's bonus to a centre back with nine clearances.
We replayed real gameweeks to validate the engine against how FPL actually scores, then
deleted the lottery on top.

### P3 · Never out of it
Competitions run as calendar-month tables. Fresh table every month, a winner every month,
join any time and you're live immediately. The season table sits behind as the prestige
board. It is never "my season's dead, see you in August" by October.

### P4 · No 2am price panic
Prices snapshot when the gameweek opens and hold all week. Nobody sets an alarm to beat a
price rise. Do your moves when you want.

### P5 · Earned power
A perfect knowledge round mints a wildcard (on top of your standard one per half-season).
Chips: Triple Captain, Bench Boost, Insight. And the armband never dies: if your captain
blanks out of the side, it passes down the chain, vice then your most in-form starter.
Team news should not delete your week.

### P6 · Actually social
FPL is a spreadsheet you check alone. YourScore Fantasy opens on a feed. Follow managers,
share your squad as a card, run private leagues with real chat, polls, a league feed.
Rate My Squad gives any squad a 0 to 10, scored by the engine on projections, fixtures,
balance and differentials, with a written verdict on top. Fantasy was always social. The
apps never were.

## 4. Formats (the machines that make debate)

- **The Grievance QT.** Find/seed a complaint about FPL (bonus points, price rises, dead
  seasons). Quote it with the fix. Never smug, always "yeah, so we deleted that rule".
- **X Polls.** One a week minimum. "Which FPL rule would you delete first?" etc. Poll
  options are our pillars wearing a disguise.
- **Rate My Squad bait.** "Drop your draft, we'll rate it out of 10, honest." Preseason we
  reply by hand in the voice; from launch the product does it in-app. This is the single
  best reply-farm we have, use it weekly.
- **Receipts posts.** Screenshot real product (squad cards via the share card, the feed,
  the rules page, Scout). Real pixels beat mockups. Never screenshot founder-gated
  surfaces as if they're live; frame as "at launch".
- **The countdown.** Final 7 days, one countdown post a day, each one carrying a different
  pillar. Countdown is the drumbeat, pillar is the content.
- **Squad-builder debates.** "£100m. Same rules you know. 15 players, max 3 a club. Who's
  your first name in?" Football debate first, product second.

## 6. Reply playbook

- First hour after any anchor: reply to everything with a pulse. The algorithm feeds
  conversation, and so does the community.
- Someone defends FPL: agree with what's true, then one line on the rule we fixed. Never
  a dunk contest with a fan. FPL players are our future users, FPL rules are the target.
- Someone posts a squad: rate it. Every time. It's on-brand and it's free content.
- Someone asks "how do transfers work": the 5/7/9/11 curve, one post, link the rules page.
- Trolls: one funny reply max, then leave it. Never explain twice.
- Never argue with a club account, a journalist, or a bereaved fanbase on a bad news day.
  Read the room before every scheduled post goes out.

## 7. Guardrails (do not cross)

1. **No fake numbers.** Real stats or no stats.
2. **No prize/cash promises.** Giveaways are retired.
3. **"Correct answers earn transfers."** Never "every answer banks one". The curve starts
   at 5 right.
4. **"Football knowledge", never "IQ".**
5. **No em/en dashes** in post copy.
6. **Don't post founder-gated surfaces as live.** Screenshots of gated features are framed
   "at launch". The public preseason CTA is gameweek-1 access via yourscore.app.
7. **Don't slag off FPL players, ever.** Rules yes, people no.
8. **If a fixture/team-news fact goes in a post, verify it that day.** Preseason facts rot
   fast.
9. **Product claims come from YOURSCORE.md.** If it's not in there, it doesn't get posted.
